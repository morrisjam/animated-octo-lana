# Wormhole Stage Brief V1

Purpose: first production brief for the gravity-well stage support pack

## What to make

- wormhole mouth reference
- swirl or flow map
- distortion mask
- emission gradient
- particle or dust atlas
- review still with fighters visible over the effect

## What it should feel like

- camera-facing aperture
- deep funnel below the playfield
- slow twisting interior motion
- cosmic, severe, and readable

## What not to make

- no planet
- no Saturn ring
- no sideways stripe
- no filled center disc
- no effect that hides the fighters

## Prompt seed

`A large camera-facing gravity-well wormhole, hollow center, slow internal twist, deep receding tunnel, electric violet and cyan glow, dark cosmic negative space, readable behind fighter silhouettes.`

## Rejection notes

- rejects any circular prop read
- rejects any horizontal band read
- rejects busy center detail
- rejects anything that breaks silhouette readability at launch zoom-out
