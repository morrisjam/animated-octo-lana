# Duelist Character Brief V1

Purpose: first production brief for the pressure fighter

## What to make

- one character concept sheet
- one turnaround or strong pose sheet
- one select portrait
- one dash or chase pose reference
- one launch and dunk pose reference
- one short note for frame readability and silhouette anchors

## What it should feel like

- fast
- sharp
- aggressive
- forward-leaning

## What not to make

- no fragile glass-cannon look
- no muddy motion blur
- no silhouette that collapses during dash or special poses
- no passive stance language

## Prompt seed

`A fast pressure fighter with a sharp forward-leaning silhouette, clear motion bias, clean chase language, and a neon cosmic combat style that stays readable over a dark wormhole arena.`

## Rejection notes

- rejects poses that lose silhouette readability
- rejects motion streaks that hide the body shape
- rejects a look that is too passive or defensive
