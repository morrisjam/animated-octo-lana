# Vanguard — Canonical Design Reference v1

Approved by James, 2026-08-04. This sheet is the identity source of truth for all Vanguard art.

## Design rules

- Single angular wing-shield with bold orange trim, mounted on HIS LEFT shoulder only — the unarmed side.
  The wing folds down over his left forearm when he blocks (parry) and plants as a full wall for the
  bastion special. Never draw it on his right.
- Crackling orange flame-lance carried in his RIGHT hand.
- Navy bio-armor of large smooth plates, minimal interior seams, no muscle striation; two or three
  glowing amber seam slits on chest/thighs only. Finned helmet, pupil-less glowing amber eyes.
- ALWAYS AIRBORNE: there is no floor in Gravity Well. Every pose in every animation state flies —
  no standing, no planted feet, no kneeling, no ground contact, no ground shadow. Legs trail or tuck,
  feet point, the body drifts or tilts. This applies to idle, attacks, guards, recovery — everything.
- Style: hand-drawn 2D action-game art — expressive brush-inked outlines with varying weight, painterly
  two-to-three-step cel shading, visible brush texture. Not vector-clean, not photoreal, not over-detailed:
  shapes must read at small sprite scale.
