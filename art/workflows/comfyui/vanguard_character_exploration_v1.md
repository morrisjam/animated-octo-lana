# Vanguard Character Exploration V1

Purpose: generate the first defensive character exploration set

## Inputs

- Vanguard brief
- heavy defensive silhouette constraints
- current visual profile target: 3d
- select-screen portrait requirements

## Outputs

- concept sheet
- front or three-quarter pose
- side or turnaround reference
- guard pose reference
- portrait candidate

## Workflow notes

- emphasize mass and protection
- keep the silhouette broad
- avoid ornate clutter
- preserve the same read from concept to portrait

## Cleanup notes

- ensure armor or body shapes do not fragment the outline
- keep the portrait and body language aligned
- reject any pose that feels light or fast
