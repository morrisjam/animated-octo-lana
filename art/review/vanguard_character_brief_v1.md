# Vanguard Character Brief V1

Purpose: first production brief for the defensive anchor fighter

## What to make

- one hero concept sheet
- one front or three-quarter turnaround
- one select portrait
- one guard pose reference
- one launch or counterpose reference
- one short note for armor, mass, and color blocking

## What it should feel like

- heavy
- deliberate
- protected
- stable under pressure

## What not to make

- no fragile silhouette
- no overly busy armor
- no playful shape language
- no motion language that makes the character look light or fast

## Prompt seed

`A heavy defensive arena fighter with a broad silhouette, controlled armor shapes, strong guard presence, stable stance, clear mass, and a cosmic sci-fi language that stays readable at game distance.`

## Rejection notes

- rejects anything that reads as generic sci-fi knight clutter
- rejects thin outlines that vanish over the wormhole background
- rejects poses that make the character feel too agile
